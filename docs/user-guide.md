# User Guide

This guide provides detailed instructions on how to use the Digital Twin Lab application. It covers the entire workflow from uploading data to generating content with your digital twin.

## Getting Started

### Creating a User Account

1. Open the Digital Twin Lab application
2. Click on "Create Account" or "New User"
3. Enter a username
4. Click "Create"

Once created, you'll be redirected to your dashboard.

## Uploading Data

To create an effective digital twin, you need to provide sample data that represents your online presence.

### Supported Data Sources

- **Twitter/X**: Tweets and replies
- **LinkedIn**: Posts and articles

### Upload Process

1. Navigate to the "Upload" section
2. Select the appropriate platform (Twitter/X or LinkedIn)
3. Choose your file(s) containing sample content
   - Text files (.txt) are preferred
   - Each file should contain multiple examples of your content
4. Click "Upload"

### File Format Guidelines

For optimal results, format your data files as follows:

**Twitter Example:**
```
Tweet 1 content #hashtags
Tweet 2 content with @mentions
Tweet 3 content with links https://example.com
```

**LinkedIn Example:**
```
LinkedIn post 1 content

LinkedIn post 2 content with multiple paragraphs
Second paragraph of post 2

LinkedIn post 3 content with #hashtags
```

## Character Card Generation

After uploading data, the system will analyze your content to generate a character card.

### Generating a Character Card

1. Navigate to the "Character Cards" section
2. Click "Generate New Card"
3. Wait while the system analyzes your data and creates your digital twin

### Understanding Your Character Card

Your character card contains:
- A personality profile based on your uploaded content
- Writing style analysis
- Topic preferences
- Tone and voice characteristics

You can view this information by clicking on the card in your dashboard.

## Content Generation

Once you have a character card, you can generate new content in your style.

### Generating Content

1. Navigate to the "Generate" section
2. Select the character card you want to use
3. Choose the platform (Twitter/X or LinkedIn)
4. Optionally, provide a specific prompt or topic
5. Click "Generate Content"

### Reviewing and Exporting Content

1. Review the generated content in the preview area
2. Use the "Regenerate" button if you want different options
3. When satisfied, you can:
   - Copy the content directly to your clipboard
   - Export it as a text file
   - Schedule it for posting (if this feature is enabled)

## Conversations

You can also have interactive conversations with your digital twin.

### Starting a Conversation

1. Navigate to the "Conversations" section
2. Select the character card you want to converse with
3. Type your message in the input box
4. Click "Send"

### Conversation Tips

- Ask open-ended questions to get more detailed responses
- Reference specific topics found in your uploaded data for more authentic responses
- Conversations are saved automatically and can be resumed later

## Managing Your Data

### Viewing Uploaded Assets

1. Navigate to the "Assets" section
2. Here you can see all your uploaded files

### Deleting Assets

1. In the "Assets" section, find the asset you want to delete
2. Click the delete icon or button
3. Confirm the deletion

Note: Deleting assets that were used to generate character cards will not delete the cards themselves.

### Managing Character Cards

1. Navigate to the "Character Cards" section
2. Here you can view, edit, or delete your character cards
3. To delete a card, click the delete icon or button and confirm

## Troubleshooting

### Upload Issues

- Ensure your files are in plain text format (.txt)
- Check that the file size is under the maximum limit (typically 5MB)
- Verify that your content examples follow the recommended format

### Generation Issues

- If content generation is taking too long, try with a smaller amount of input data
- If generated content doesn't match your style, try uploading more diverse examples
- Ensure your character card was successfully created before attempting to generate content

### Account Issues

- If you cannot access your account, verify you're using the correct user ID
- Contact support if you need to recover or reset your account

## Best Practices

- Upload at least 10-20 examples of your content for the best results
- Include a variety of topics and styles in your samples
- Regularly update your digital twin with new content to keep it current
- Use specific prompts when generating content for more targeted results

## FAQ

**Q: How much data do I need to upload?**
A: We recommend at least 10-20 examples of your content for good results, but more examples will generally lead to a more accurate digital twin.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, your data is stored securely and is only used to train your personal digital twin.

**Q: Can I create multiple digital twins?**
A: Yes, you can create multiple character cards for different purposes or personas.

**Q: How accurate is the generated content?**
A: The accuracy depends on the quality and quantity of your uploaded samples. More diverse and numerous examples lead to better results.

**Q: Can I edit generated content?**
A: Yes, you can copy and edit any generated content before using it elsewhere.
